# NMIT visual review

Date: 17 September 2026. URL: https://nmit-solutions.com/

Inspected desktop 1280 × 720 and mobile 390 × 844, including the banner, integration explanation, service cards, customer logos, statistics, capability grid, blog, contact, and footer.

The layout uses a fixed white header, photographic banners with blue/cyan overlays, bold centered headings, circular gradient icons, white service sections, and a pale blog background.

## Findings

- No primary action appears in the introductory banner.
- Fixed-header anchor positioning partially obscures some headings.
- Service cards offer category labels without detail or links.
- Careers leads to company capabilities rather than recruitment information.
- Blog and utility links use placeholder fragment destinations.
- Three different social symbols link to the same LinkedIn address.
- Mobile menu remains open after selecting a section.
- Mobile document width is 412px at a 390px viewport because the map iframe is 400px wide.
- Meaningful company/client logos lack descriptive alternative text.
- Contact fields rely on placeholders; the submit action has limited prominence.

Screenshots were captured and inspected in-session. No quantitative visual score or performance score is assigned. Detailed business/content findings and coverage limits are in `NMIT-WEBSITE-REVIEW.md`.
