Cookies & browser storage

How NMIT uses local browser storage, fonts, and external services.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

What this website stores

This site uses a browser local-storage entry named nmit-theme to remember your light or dark appearance choice. Its value is light or dark. It remains on your device until you clear site data or your browser removes it. It does not contain your name, email address, or project details.

Cookies and tracking

The website application does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request information to deliver and protect the website. This notice does not describe cookies on external sites you choose to visit.

Your choices

You can change the theme using the appearance button in the header. To remove the saved preference, clear this website’s site data in your browser settings. The website continues to work without the preference; it follows your device appearance setting.

Fonts and outbound links

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. Links to Google Maps, LinkedIn, and technical reference sites open those providers’ services. Their own privacy notices apply to your use of those services.

Enquiry drafts

Contact form values are used to prepare an email draft on your device. The website application does not send these values to a server or save them in local storage. You choose whether to send the email using your email application.
