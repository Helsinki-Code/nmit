A straightforward
privacy notice.

This notice describes the behavior of this website and its enquiry tools.

Email enquiries

The contact form prepares an email on your device. The website does not upload or store the form contents. You choose whether to send the prepared email from your own email application.

If you send an enquiry, the details you include are received by the NMIT business email address. Do not include passwords, payment information, or confidential project material in an initial enquiry.

Website delivery and request information

Our hosting provider processes requests to deliver the site. Request information may include your IP address, browser information, requested URL, and request time. Hosting and security logs are separate from the contact form; the form itself does not submit its contents to the website server.

Your theme preference

The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage. This preference stays on your device and can be removed by clearing site data.

Fonts and external services

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. Those requests disclose ordinary connection information, including your IP address, to the font provider. The site does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Links to LinkedIn and Google Maps take you to external websites with their own privacy practices. No map is embedded on this site.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without completing the contact form and clear the saved theme choice in your browser. For questions or requests concerning personal information you have emailed to NMIT, use the contact address below and describe your request. Please do not send identity documents in an initial message.

See our cookies and browser storage notice for details about local storage and external services. Last updated: 17 September 2026.

Questions about personal information

Contact info@nmit-solutions.com about information you have sent to the company. Company-wide retention periods and other data-handling arrangements should be confirmed directly with NMIT.
