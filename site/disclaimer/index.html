Content & illustration notice

How to interpret NMIT engineering guides, examples, illustrations, and third-party references.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Engineering guides

Articles explain general architecture and operational concepts. Their examples do not assess your particular environment or replace project-specific requirements, security review, testing, or vendor documentation. Confirm assumptions against the systems you operate before applying an example.

Examples and imagery

Worked examples identified as hypothetical are not customer case studies. Nodi is a fictional NMIT mascot. Mascot artwork and conceptual illustrations are AI-generated brand assets, not photographs of employees, actual product screenshots, or evidence of a delivered customer engagement.

Names, logos, and references

Company names and third-party logos identify the organizations or tools being discussed. They remain the property of their respective owners. A reference or illustration does not by itself establish endorsement, certification, partnership, or a particular customer result.

Commercial information

Service descriptions introduce topics for a direct conversation. Project scope, fees, availability, staffing, and support commitments are confirmed separately. Contact NMIT for current details rather than treating an article example as a quotation.

Corrections

If you find an error, an outdated reference, or an issue with an image or attribution, email info@nmit-solutions.com with the page address and the specific issue.
